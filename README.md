## Overview

Welcome to the source repository for my personal academic portfolio website. I am a Ph.D. Research Scholar in Astronomy & Astrophysics at the **Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology (IIST)**, Thiruvananthapuram, India, working under the supervision of **Dr. Anand Narayanan**.

My research focuses on observational probes of the **Circumgalactic Medium (CGM)** and **Intergalactic Medium (IGM)** to decode the cosmic baryon cycle around galaxies. I combine high-resolution far-ultraviolet space spectroscopy from the *Hubble Space Telescope* (*HST*/COS) with optical 3D integral field unit observations (*VLT*/MUSE), photoionization modeling (`Cloudy`), and cosmological magnetohydrodynamical simulations (**IllustrisTNG** & **EAGLE**).

---

## Interactive Features on the Live Site

The portfolio is designed as an interactive astrophysics laboratory and telemetry deck:

- 🌌 **Live Cosmic Web Canvas**: Real-time 60fps hydrodynamical simulation of cold-mode filamentary accretion ($T \sim 10^4\text{ K}$) and bipolar feedback plumes ($T \sim 10^{5.5\text{--}6.0}\text{ K}$) feeding a galaxy pair halo, complete with live telemetry coordinates and impact parameter tracking ($\rho/R_{\text{vir}}$).
- 🎬 **Cosmological Simulations Theatre**:
  - **Stream 01**: 8-panel diagnostic field inspector of IllustrisTNG TNG100 Halo #194946 ([Zinger et al. 2020](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/499/1/768/5905089)) with independent playback speed controls ($0.5\times$ to $2\times$).
  - **Stream 02**: Interactive Quasar Sightline & *HST*/COS Voigt Spectrometer with presets from published papers, including the LBQS 0107$-$0235 partial Lyman limit system and PKS 0405$-$123 / HE 0226$-$4110 pair halo.
  - **Stream 03**: Simulation Atlas comparing high-resolution gas accretion in TNG50 ([Nelson et al. 2020](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article-abstract/498/2/2391/5900540)), collimated bipolar outflows ([Nelson et al. 2019](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/490/3/3234/5556547); [Pillepich et al. 2019](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/490/3/3196/5566345)), and the Durham ICC **EAGLE** time-evolution suite.
- 🧮 **In-Browser $\Lambda$CDM Cosmology Workbench**: Real-time Simpson quadrature numerical integrator for cosmic lookback times, comoving / angular diameter / luminosity distances, and angular scales at absorber redshifts, alongside links to the companion native Android Flutter release.
- 📚 **Academic Output & CV**: Complete publication ledger with expandable abstract drawers, one-click BibTeX copy, PDF curriculum vitae download, and public astronomy outreach log.
